      Screen brightness

      The light sensor measures how bright the room is (in lux) and the screen follows it smoothly: in a dark room it uses the Darkest room brightness, in a bright room the Brightest room brightness, and in between something in the middle. Change the two sliders if the screen feels too bright at night or too dim during the day.

      Advanced

      For reference: a dark bedroom is under 1 lux, a living room in the evening about 50–150 lux, a bright room by day 300–1000 lux. Below the "dark" level the screen stays at the darkest brightness, above the "bright" level at the brightest. Turning the screen off below a level makes it go dark when the lights are off; a touch wakes it for a minute.
