Albums

To add an album: in Synology Photos open the album, choose Share, turn on the public link, copy it and paste it here.

Connection to the NAS

Leave this on. Turn it off only if your share links use the NAS's local address (like https://192.168.1.10:5001/…) and adding an album fails with a certificate error.

Slideshow

Short videos

Videos in your albums are not part of the slideshow. When someone swipes to the next photo, the frame first plays a short video (without sound), then shows the next photo. Swiping again skips the video.

Videos longer than one minute are never used.

Clock & weather

Add one city, or up to three to see the time and weather in several places. The first city is the main one: it is shown large, with the date and forecast. Use ↑ to choose which city comes first.

      Brightness

      A light sensor measures how bright the room is, and the screen follows it: dim in a dark room, bright in a bright one.

      1. Show the frame what your room looks like. The sensor sees more or less light depending on where it is mounted. Tap It's dark now in the evening, with the lights you usually have on, and It's bright now on a bright day. Tap Save after each.

      2. Choose how bright the screen gets. If it's still too bright at night or too dim during the day, move these.

      More brightness options

      Light is measured in lux. For comparison: a dark bedroom is under 1 lux, a living room in the evening about 50–150, a bright room during the day 300–1000. The two buttons above fill in the first two values for you.
